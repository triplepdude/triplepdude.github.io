It also removes the combining grapheme joiner U+034F, the Hangul fillers, and variation selectors that come in runs, follow no character or follow one this tool removes or replaces. ## What is kept on purpose - **Emoji sequences.** A joiner between two emoji (a family, a rainbow flag) stays, and so do the tag characters that spell a region inside a flag such as Scotland's. Tags that spell anything else are removed. - **Scripts that need joiners.** A joiner between two letters of Persian, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali and similar scripts changes how the word is written, so it stays. Between Latin letters it goes. - **Old-style Hangul syllables** keep a filler that stands in for a missing part, and **visible format characters**, such as the Arabic number signs U+0600 to U+0605, are left alone. Collapsing spaces keeps the indentation at the start of a line. ## How em dashes are replaced With **comma**, "the result—surprisingly—was good" becomes "the result, surprisingly, was good". A dash between two numbers, or in a range such as 2019—present, becomes a hyphen; a dash right before other punctuation is dropped; a dash at the start of a line becomes a hyphen. **Hyphen with spaces** puts one space on each side of the hyphen, and **plain hyphen** keeps the spacing. Read the result once: an em dash sometimes works as a colon, and no rule can know that. ## Markdown and NFC **Remove Markdown formatting**, off by default, takes out the marks chat assistants use, so text pasted into an email, form or Word document reads cleanly. Bold, italic and strikethrough marks, heading marks, backticks, code fence lines, quote marks and horizontal rules go. A link becomes its text with the address in brackets, an image its alt text, and `-`, `*` or `+` bullets become • or disappear, as you choose. Numbered lists, tables and asterisks with spaces around them, as in 2 * 3, are left alone. **Normalise to NFC** joins a letter and a separate accent mark (e followed by U+0301) into the single character é that most software expects. The text looks the same, but search, sorting and word counts work better. It also replaces a few compatibility characters, such as the angstrom sign U+212B and CJK compatibility ideographs, with their standard forms. ## Hidden characters and AI detectors Invisible characters can come from typography, from the app you copied from, or from deliberate tampering: tag characters mirror printable ASCII and have been used to hide instructions in text given to AI tools, so the highlighted view decodes them.
