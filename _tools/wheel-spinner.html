Blank lines are skipped, and an entry written twice gets two slices and twice the chance. Press **Spin** or click the wheel. When it stops, a pop-up shows the winner, and **Remove from wheel** takes that line out of the list before the next spin. Results are listed under the wheel, and **Copy results** copies them all. **Quick lists** load yes or no, yes, no or maybe, or the numbers 1 to 10. **Shuffle** and **Sort A–Z** reorder the box, and **Undo** brings back the list as it was before the last change made with a button. You can also open or drop a plain text file with one entry per line. ## Is the wheel random, and can it be rigged? The winner is drawn the moment you press Spin, before the wheel moves, by your browser's cryptographically secure generator (`crypto.getRandomValues`). The animation then turns the wheel to that slice; the spin time, the colours and the moment you click change nothing. Each slice's size is its exact chance, so with 8 equal entries each has 1 in 8 (12.5%), and a weighted entry's bigger slice shows its odds to everyone watching. The usual shortcut, a random 32-bit number modulo the number of entries, slightly favours the first entries whenever 2³² is not a multiple of that number. This page takes only the random bits it needs and draws again when the value is too large, so no entry is favoured. ## Weights Tick **Use weights** and end a line with `*` and a number to change its chance. A tab before the number works too, so you can paste two columns from a spreadsheet. A line without a weight counts as 1. | Line | Weight | Chance | |---|---|---| | `Pizza *3` | 3 | 1 in 2 (50%) | | `Sushi *2` | 2 | 1 in 3 (33.33%) | | `Tacos` | 1 | 1 in 6 (16.67%) | A weight is a whole number or a decimal with up to two places, from 0.01 to 1000. `*1.5` and `*1,5` both mean one and a half. Thousands separators are not accepted, so write `*1000`, not `*1,000`. ## Picking without repeats To pick everyone once in a random order, remove each winner after its spin. With equal weights every order is then equally likely, as when names are drawn from a hat. Without removing, a wheel of 30 names needs about 120 spins on average before every name has come up. ## Saving and sharing The page saves nothing in cookies or browser storage. The list and the settings live in the page address after the `#`, which browsers never send to a server, so a bookmark or the link from **Copy link** opens the same wheel. The address holds up to 16,000 characters, which is 1,600 names of seven letters; accented letters and other scripts take more room. A longer list still spins, and the page tells you that the link cannot hold it. To show the link to a room, make a code with the [QR code generator](/qr-code-generator/). ## Limits and options - Up to 10,000 entries and 300,000 characters. Names on slices too thin to label are not written on the wheel but win like any other. - Spin time from 1 to 30 seconds. The tick and win sounds are made by your browser, not downloaded. - **Full screen** shows only the wheel, its Spin button and the result. Safari on iPhone does not let pages go full screen, so there the wheel fills the browser window instead. - If your device is set to reduce motion, the wheel stops at once unless you tick **Animate the spin**. For a number from any range, use the [random number generator](/random-number-generator/); to give each turn a time limit, the [online timer](/online-timer/). faq: - q: Why did the same name come up twice in a row? a: Every spin is a new draw and the wheel has no memory, so with 8 entries the last winner wins again 1 time in 8. If a name should not win twice, press Remove from wheel after it is picked. - q: Does a longer spin or clicking at the right moment change the result? a: No. The winner is drawn as soon as you press Spin, and the animation only shows the wheel turning to it. On a touch screen, tap the wheel to spin it. - q: Does the wheel work without an internet connection? a: Once the page has loaded, yes. The wheel, the sounds and the draw need no connection.
