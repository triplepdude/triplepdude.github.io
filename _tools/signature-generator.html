Nothing is uploaded. category: Image & PDF keywords: [signature generator, draw signature online, type signature online, online signature maker, transparent signature png, signature from photo, remove background from signature, electronic signature] howto: | ## Three ways to make your signature - **Draw:** sign in the box with a mouse, a finger or a stylus. **Undo** (or Ctrl+Z) removes the last stroke and **Clear** starts over; Undo also brings a cleared signature back. - **Type:** enter your name and pick one of five script fonts: Dancing Script, Great Vibes, Sacramento, Herr Von Muellerhoff and Mrs Saint Delafield. Each option shows your own name, and a size slider sets how large it is. - **Upload photo:** sign a sheet of white paper, photograph or scan it and open the picture. The paper is removed and the ink kept, as a transparent PNG. Choose black or blue ink or any colour for all three, then download a **PNG** with a transparent background or an **SVG** vector file. Both are trimmed to the ink with a little padding, so they drop neatly onto a signature line. ## Tips for each method - When drawing, sign at your normal speed. **Natural line width** thins fast strokes and thickens slow ones, like a ballpoint; a stylus (Apple Pencil, Surface Pen, S Pen) uses pen pressure instead. A finger on a phone usually looks better than a mouse. - For a photo, use daylight or even lighting, fill the frame with the signature and keep the edge of the paper and the table out of the picture. A dark pen gives the cleanest result. - Some offices ask for blue ink, because it makes an original easy to tell apart from a black-and-white copy. ## How the photo cleanup works Phone photos of paper are never evenly white: one corner is in shadow, the other catches the light. So the tool first estimates the paper's brightness around every point, from the brightest pixels in small blocks, smoothed across the page. Each pixel is then compared with its own local paper, which removes shadows that a single threshold would turn into ink. **Paper cut-off** sets how dark a pixel must be, relative to the paper, to count as ink. The automatic value starts from Otsu's method, which splits the brightness histogram into its two most distinct groups, and leans a little towards the paper so light strokes survive. Lower it if paper texture shows, raise it if thin strokes break up. Tiny isolated specks (dust, grain, JPEG noise) are removed, and the result is cropped to the ink. The ink can be recoloured or keep its own colour. ## PNG or SVG? | Feature | PNG | SVG | |---|---|---| | What it stores | Pixels, transparent background | Vector curves | | Enlarging | Blurs beyond its pixel size | Sharp at any size | | Good for | Word, Google Docs, PDF editors, email | Design tools, websites | {: .data .sg-table} The PNG of a drawn or typed signature is drawn at twice its on-screen size by default; choose 3× or 4× for large prints. Turn on **White background** if an app shows transparent areas as black. A typed signature's SVG holds the letters as outlines, so it needs no font installed. Photos have no SVG, because they are pixels. ## Can I use it to sign documents? Many legal systems accept electronic signatures for everyday agreements: the US ESIGN Act and the EU eIDAS Regulation both say a signature cannot be denied legal effect just because it is electronic. Some documents, such as wills or notarised deeds, still need ink or a certified digital signature, so check what the recipient accepts. Most PDF editors, including the Fill & Sign tool in Adobe Acrobat Reader, can add an image as your signature. - q: Can I use the typed script fonts in my documents? a: Yes. Lower values remove more of the paper, including shadows and texture; higher values keep fainter ink. If the edge of the paper or the table is in the picture, crop the photo closer to the signature and open it again. - q: Why does my signature appear on a black background? a: The PNG background is transparent, and a few apps and image viewers display transparency as black. Turn on White background before downloading, or use the SVG file. ---
