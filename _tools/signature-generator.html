Turn on **White background** if an app shows the transparent areas as black. ## How it works The pad is an HTML canvas that listens to Pointer Events, the W3C standard that delivers mouse, touch and pen input through one interface, including pen pressure. Each sample is stored with its position and a width factor, and strokes are drawn as quadratic Bézier curves through the midpoints between samples, which turns the jagged line reported by the hardware into smooth curves. The canvas matches your screen's pixel density, so lines are crisp on high-resolution displays, and scrolling is switched off inside the pad so a finger stroke does not move the page. Because the strokes are kept as vectors, each download is redrawn from them at the size you pick, and the SVG contains the same curves as editable paths. ## Can I use it to sign documents? Many legal systems accept electronic signatures for everyday agreements: the US ESIGN Act and the EU eIDAS Regulation both say a signature cannot be denied legal effect just because it is electronic. Some documents, such as wills or notarised deeds, still need ink or a certified digital signature, so check what the recipient accepts.
