Page numbers count from the start of the file. ## Which resolution to choose A PDF measures pages in points, 72 to the inch, so a page's width in pixels is its width in inches times the DPI (dots per inch). | DPI | A4 page, pixels | US Letter, pixels | Good for | |---|---|---|---| | 72 | 595 × 842 | 612 × 792 | Previews and thumbnails | | 150 | 1240 × 1754 | 1275 × 1650 | Screens, email, slides | | 300 | 2480 × 3508 | 2550 × 3300 | Printing | | 600 | 4961 × 7016 | 5100 × 6600 | Small print, zooming in | Each thumbnail shows the size its page will have. Doubling the DPI makes four times as many pixels, so files get much larger. The DPI is also written into each image, so a word processor or print dialog that reads it shows the page at its real size. A very large page, such as a poster, can need more pixels than one image may have here: 64 million on a computer, 36 million on a touch screen device. It is then converted at the highest DPI that fits, and the tool tells you which. ## JPG or PNG? **JPG** files are smaller and suit photos, scans and email. They lose a little detail: at quality 90, the default, the loss is hard to see, while much lower settings leave smudges around the edges of text. **PNG** keeps every pixel, which keeps text and line drawings crisp, but makes larger files for photos. Only PNG can be transparent: tick **Transparent background** to leave the parts of the page where nothing is drawn see through, which suits logos and diagrams. Pages that paint their own white background stay white. ## Why is the JPG blurry? Usually the resolution is too low for how the image is used: a 72 DPI image looks soft when it is enlarged or printed. Choose 300 DPI for print. A scanned PDF holds a picture of each page, so converting it above the resolution it was scanned at makes bigger files, not sharper ones. Low JPG quality blurs fine text too; use 90 or more, or PNG. Pages are drawn by PDF.js, the PDF engine of Firefox, as a PDF viewer shows them, with annotations and filled-in form fields. Fonts that are not embedded in the PDF are replaced with similar ones.
