It runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. category: Image & PDF keywords: [rotate pdf, rotate pdf pages, how to rotate a pdf, rotate pdf and save, rotate pdf permanently, rotate one page in pdf, flip pdf, rotate pdf online free] howto: | ## How to rotate a PDF and save it 1. Press **Choose PDF files** or drop one or more PDFs on the page. Every page appears as a thumbnail, shown the way it will be saved. 2. Turn a single page with the buttons under it: ↺ turns it 90° to the left, ↻ 90° to the right, and **180°** flips it upside down. 3. To turn many pages at once, select them and use **Rotate selected**, or use **Rotate all**. **Undo** takes back the last change and **Reset** puts every page back as it was. 4. Press **Save rotated PDF**. One PDF is saved as a PDF, several are saved together in a ZIP file. Click a page to select it, Shift+click to select every page up to it, or drag across several. On a touch screen, tap a page, or touch and hold one and then drag. **All**, **Odd**, **Even**, **Portrait** and **Landscape** select pages for you: **Even** and **Rotate selected 180°** fix a scan whose back pages came out upside down. With a keyboard, the arrow keys move between pages, Space selects, R turns right, Shift+R turns left and Ctrl+Z undoes. ## Why a PDF opens sideways again The rotate button of a PDF viewer, such as **View > Rotate view** in Adobe Acrobat Reader, only turns the page on your screen and is not saved, so the file opens sideways again next time. A PDF keeps each page's turn in the file itself, as a number of degrees clockwise in steps of 90. This tool changes that number for the pages you turned, so every viewer and printer shows them the new way. | Button | The page turns | A page at 0° is saved at | |---|---|---| | ↻ | 90° clockwise | 90° | | ↺ | 90° anticlockwise | 270° | | 180° | upside down | 180° | ## Pages with sideways text The tool reads which way the text runs on each page and marks the pages where it runs sideways or upside down, such as a landscape table on a portrait page. **Turn them upright** turns all of those pages so their text reads normally. A scan without a text layer (one whose text you cannot select) has no text to read, so check those pages by eye. ## What the saved file keeps Only the turn of each page you changed is written. It is added to the end of the file as an *incremental update*, usually a few hundred bytes for each page, while the original bytes stay as they are. Nothing is redrawn or compressed again, so text stays sharp and searchable, pictures keep every pixel, and bookmarks, links, form fields, tags, attachments and document properties are kept. The [PDF merger](/merge-pdf/) can turn pages too, but it copies them into a new file without the bookmarks, and form fields may stop being fillable. To take pages out of a file instead, use [split PDF](/split-pdf/). A damaged file, whose index of objects no longer matches its contents, is saved again in full instead, which also repairs it. faq: - q: Are my PDFs uploaded to a server? a: No. The files are read, previewed and saved by JavaScript in this page, on your own device. Nothing is sent over the network or stored, so confidential and password-protected documents stay with you. - q: Can I rotate a password-protected or signed PDF? a: Yes. Type the password when asked; the rotated copy keeps the same password, encryption and restrictions, and a PDF whose permissions forbid changing its pages needs its permissions password. A signed PDF keeps the signed version inside the file, so the signature still checks out for that version, but readers show that pages changed after signing. A certified PDF allows no page changes, so rotating breaks its certification. - q: Can I rotate a PDF on my phone? a: Yes. The page works in the browser of a phone or tablet, with no app to install; tap the buttons under a page to turn it, then save. On an iPhone or iPad it needs iOS 18 or later, and on Android Chrome 125 or later. A file of several hundred megabytes may not fit in a phone's memory, so turn those on a computer. - q: Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality? a: No. Rotating only changes the number that tells viewers which way up to show each page. The text, pictures and drawings on the page are not touched, so they stay exactly as sharp as before. - q: Can I rotate a page by 45 degrees, or straighten a crooked scan? a: No. A PDF can turn a page only in steps of 90 degrees. Straightening a scan that is a few degrees off means redrawing the picture on the page, which this tool does not do. ---
