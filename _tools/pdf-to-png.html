Runs in your browser. category: Image & PDF keywords: [pdf to png, convert pdf to png, pdf to png converter, how to convert pdf to png, pdf to transparent png, pdf to png high resolution, pdf page to png, extract images from pdf] howto: | ## How to convert a PDF to PNG Choose a PDF, or drop one on the page; a PDF that needs a password asks for it, and the password stays on your device. Every page appears as a thumbnail and all of them are ticked. Pick a size and press **Convert**: one page is saved as a PNG file, several are saved together in a ZIP file. To convert just one page, type its number in **Pages to convert**, or press **Select none** and click or tap the page. **Copy image** then puts the page on the clipboard, ready to paste into a slide, a document or a chat. On a phone, **Share** sends the converted pages to another app. ## Which DPI or width to choose A PDF measures pages in points, 72 to the inch, so a page's width in pixels is its width in inches times the DPI (dots per inch). | Use | Setting | A4 page | US Letter page | |---|---|---|---| | Web pages, email | 150 DPI | 1240 × 1754 | 1275 × 1650 | | Slides, Full HD screens | 1920 pixels wide | 1920 × 2715 | 1920 × 2485 | | Printing | 300 DPI | 2480 × 3508 | 2550 × 3300 | | Small print, zooming in | 600 DPI | 4961 × 7016 | 5100 × 6600 | Each thumbnail shows the size its page will have. The DPI is stored in each page's PNG, so a word processor or print dialog that reads it shows the page at its real size. A poster-sized page is made at the highest DPI that fits in 64 million pixels, or 36 million on a touch screen. ## Transparent PNG and trimmed margins **Transparent background** leaves see-through the parts of a page where nothing is drawn. Many PDFs paint each page white first, so for those choose **Make white transparent**: every pixel becomes the most see-through colour that looks the same on white. Black text keeps its soft edges, grey becomes half-transparent black, and pale colours turn partly see-through too. In black and white, only the black is kept. **Trim margins** cuts away the blank edges and keeps only what is drawn, for logos, signatures and figures. With a pixel width, the trimmed image gets exactly that width. ## Why is my PNG so big? PNG keeps every pixel, so text stays crisp, but photos make large files: a page of text comes out about the size of a JPG of quality 90, a page of photos three to four times bigger. For pages that are mostly photos, [PDF to JPG](/pdf-to-jpg/) makes far smaller files. Otherwise, choose fewer colours: | Colours | Compared with Exact | Good for | |---|---|---| | Exact | The browser's own PNG | Everything | | 256 colours | Usually 55% to 75% smaller | Charts, slides, text | | Greyscale | Usually 55% to 70% smaller | Documents without colour | | Black and white | Usually 85% to 98% smaller | Text and line drawings | With 256 colours and in black and white, the pictures on a page are dithered (mixed from dots of the colours there are), so photos show no bands; text and lines are not, so they stay sharp. ## Pictures in the PDF Choose **Pictures in the PDF** to save the photos and images inside the chosen pages, each at its own pixel size, instead of whole pages. A picture that appears on several pages is saved once. Text, and drawings made of lines and shapes, are not pictures.
