Choose images, drop them on the box or paste one with Ctrl+V (⌘V on a Mac). JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, ICO, TIFF, iPhone HEIC and SVG all work, and you can add many at once. 2. Click a file name in the list to preview that image. ## Exact sizes: fit, crop, pad or stretch When the aspect ratio is unlocked, a size such as 1080 × 1080 rarely has your photo's shape, so you choose what happens: | Mode | Output size | What you see | |---|---|---| | Fit inside | At most W × H | The whole image, shape kept | | Crop to fill | Exactly W × H | Covers the frame; the overflow is cut | | Pad | Exactly W × H | The whole image on a background colour | | Stretch | Exactly W × H | Distorted to the new shape | {: .data .ri-table} The 3 × 3 **position** grid picks which part a crop keeps (the top of a portrait, say) or where a padded image sits. Pad with white, black, a colour of your choice or transparency. **Never enlarge** leaves images that are already smaller than the target at their own size, which is what you want when shrinking a mixed batch to a maximum width. ## Resizing to a file size in KB Upload forms often cap the size, for example at 50 KB. Enter the limit and the tool searches for the highest JPEG, WebP or AVIF quality that fits, by encoding the image several times. If even quality 40 is too big, it also reduces the width and height, keeping the shape, and tells you the new size. Exact-size modes keep their pixel size and go down to quality 5 instead. PNG and BMP are lossless, so only the size can shrink. The limit counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes, so the file also passes checks that count 1,024. ## How the resizing works Your browser decodes each image, turning phone photos upright from their EXIF orientation tag, and draws it at the new size with its best smoothing filter. For big reductions each side is halved step by step until it is at most twice its target, then scaled once more. Every halving averages neighbouring pixels, so fine detail such as hair, text and fabric does not break up into jagged edges and moiré. SVG drawings are rendered straight at the new size, so they stay sharp at any scale. Enlarging a photo cannot add detail: the new pixels are estimated from their neighbours, so edges soften. ## Formats | Format | Transparency | Best for | |---|---|---| | JPEG | No, filled with the background | Photos, smallest files | | PNG | Yes | Screenshots, logos, text | | WebP, AVIF | Yes | Web pages; AVIF is smallest but slowest to save | | BMP | No | Old software; 24-bit, uncompressed | | ICO | Yes | Icons up to 256 × 256, optionally with 16, 32 and 48 px sizes | {: .data .ri-table} Most browsers cannot write AVIF, so a bundled libavif encoder does it in your browser; it loads (3.5 MB) the first time and takes a few seconds per image. HEIC, TIFF and GIF inputs are saved as JPEG (HEIC) or PNG under "Original format". The new files carry no metadata, so the camera model and GPS location of a photo are not copied. faq: - q: Are my images uploaded to a server? a: No. Every image is decoded, resized and encoded by your own browser, and the ZIP is built on your device.
