Runs in your browser. category: Device Tests keywords: [cps test, click speed test, clicks per second test, cps test 5 seconds, cps test 10 seconds, mouse click test, double click test, kohi click test, right click cps test, spacebar cps test, jitter click test, butterfly click test] howto: | ## How to use the CPS test Choose a test length and what to count, then click the box as fast as you can. The timer starts with your first click, not when the page loads, and the count stops the moment the time is up. Your score is clicks divided by seconds: 52 clicks in the 5-second test is 10.40 CPS. - **Left click or tap** counts the main mouse button, touchpad clicks and taps on a touch screen, one for each finger. - **Right click** counts the right button and stops the menu from opening inside the box. It needs a mouse or touchpad: taps on a touch screen are not counted in this mode. - **Space bar** counts presses while the box has the focus. Holding the key down counts once, because auto-repeat is ignored. If the bar seems to type twice, the [keyboard tester](/keyboard-tester/) checks for key chatter. To count with no time limit, or to measure the key's repeat delay and rate, use the [spacebar counter](/spacebar-counter/). Your best score for each length and button is kept until you reload or close the page. To open a set-up directly, add it to the address, for example `#10s`, `#60s-right` or `#5s-space`. ## What is a good CPS? The Guinness World Record for the most mouse clicks in one minute is 760, an average of 12.67 clicks per second, set by Yiğit Arslan in Istanbul on 10 February 2026. Short tests give higher scores because fingers tire quickly: in a 2024 study, people tapping a screen as fast as they could slowed by about 17% within 30 seconds. Only compare scores from tests of the same length. The rank shown with your score is this page's own scale: under 4 CPS is *warming up*, 4 to 6 *steady*, 6 to 8 *quick*, 8 to 11 *fast*, 11 to 14 *very fast* and 14 or more *extreme*. ## Jitter, butterfly and drag clicking | Technique | How it works | CPS players usually report | |---|---|---| | Normal | One finger presses and lets go | 6 to 9 | | Jitter | Tense the forearm so the finger vibrates on the button | 10 to 14 | | Butterfly | Index and middle finger hit one button in turn | 15 to 25 | | Drag | Slide a dry fingertip along the button so it skips | 25 or more, in bursts | Butterfly and drag clicking also depend on the mouse. A mouse ignores a second press that comes within its debounce time, which filters out the bounce of the switch contacts, so a mouse with a long debounce time counts fewer of these clicks. Some gaming mice let you change it in their software. ## Does CPS matter in Minecraft PvP? It depends on the edition. Since Java Edition 1.9 (2016) an attack cooldown scales damage by the time since your last swing, and a sword needs 0.625 seconds to recharge fully, so spam clicking lands weak hits. Bedrock Edition and servers that keep 1.8 combat have no cooldown, so a higher CPS gives you more chances to hit the moment the target's half-second damage immunity ends. Minecraft players also call a click speed test a Kohi click test, after a PvP server whose website had one. To test how fast you react rather than how fast you click, try the [reaction time test](/reaction-time-test/). faq: - q: Is my score saved or sent anywhere? a: No. Clicks are counted by JavaScript in this page and kept only in memory, so your scores are gone when you reload or close the tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored in your browser or tracked. - q: Can an auto-clicker get me banned? a: On most multiplayer servers, yes. Hypixel's rules forbid auto-clickers, burst-click buttons and click macros, and server anti-cheat systems can flag clicking that is too fast or too regular to be human. Before you use a mouse's rapid-fire or macro feature, check the rules of the server you play on. - q: Why does my mouse double-click when I click once? a: A mechanical mouse switch has metal contacts that bounce for a moment when pressed, and the mouse ignores that bounce for a short debounce time. When the switch wears out or gets dirty, the bounce lasts longer and one press is reported as two. To check, start a 10-second test and click slowly, about once a second; if the counter ever jumps by two, the switch is failing. - q: Does the CPS test work on a phone, tablet or touchpad? a: Yes. In left-click mode every tap on a touch screen counts, so two fingers tapping in turn both count. On a touchpad, taps and presses count as left clicks, and a two-finger tap usually counts as a right click. ---
