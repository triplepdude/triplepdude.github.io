WhatsApp stickers are WebP as well: 512 × 512 pixels on a transparent background. PNG is the safer choice for editing and passing on. Clear pixels stay clear, and the half-transparent pixels along soft edges, shadows and smoothed lettering keep their precise opacity. A sticker or logo converted here can be laid over any background. ## Why the PNG is bigger Most WebP photos are lossy: they were made small by discarding detail. PNG has no such shortcut. It stores every decoded pixel exactly, with lossless compression, and in tests with sample photos saved as WebP at 80%, the PNG came out 11 to 15 times larger. The picture looks no different, because the conversion neither repairs nor damages it. A lossless WebP, usual for logos and graphics, grows much less: Google measures lossless WebP files at 26% smaller than PNGs on average. Each finished file shows its new size and the change in percent.
