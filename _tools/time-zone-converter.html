CST, MST, PST and other standard-time abbreviations work the same way. ## Why is London sometimes only four hours ahead of New York? The US changes its clocks on the second Sunday of March and the first Sunday of November, the UK and the EU on the last Sundays of March and October. In between, the gap is 4 hours instead of 5: in 2027 from 14 to 28 March and from 31 October to 7 November. The weekly table shows how a meeting's local times move through the year. ## Skipped and repeated times When clocks go forward, an hour is skipped: 02:30 on 14 March 2027 does not exist in New York, so the page shows that moment as 03:30 EDT. When they go back, an hour repeats: 01:30 on 7 November 2027 happens twice, and you pick which one you mean. ## When can London, New York and India all meet? No hour falls between 9:00 and 17:00 in all three. The usual compromise is early morning in New York: in winter 8:00 EST is 13:00 in London and 18:30 in India, in summer 8:00 EDT is 13:00 BST and 17:30 IST. ## Calendar files and links **Download .ics** saves the meeting for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar with its times in UTC, so every guest sees it in their own time zone. UTC is the world's reference time scale, kept by atomic clocks, while GMT is also the name of the UK's winter time. From the last Sunday of March to the last Sunday of October the UK is on BST, UTC+01:00, so a summer time given in GMT is an hour behind UK clocks." - q: What does AoE mean in a deadline? a: "Anywhere on Earth: UTC−12:00, the last time zone in which a date ends.
