Runs in your browser. category: Date & Time keywords: [date calculator, days between dates, days between two dates, add days to date, how many days until, business days calculator, working days between dates, days from today, date difference calculator] howto: | ## How to count the days between two dates Choose a start date and an end date. The answer appears at once as days, as weeks and days, as years, months and days and as working days, with the weekday of each date. The start is today until you change it, so the same tab answers "how many days until" a date. The buttons under the dates fill in the next [Christmas](#to=christmas), [New Year's Day](#to=new-year), [Easter](#to=easter), [Halloween](#to=halloween) or US [Thanksgiving](#to=thanksgiving). To move a date instead, open **Add or subtract days**: pick a start date, enter years, months, weeks or days, and read off the new date. Adding 45 days to 14 November 2026 gives 29 December 2026. ## Is the end date included? Normally not. From Monday to Friday of one week is four days, because you count the nights in between. Ages, countdowns and hotel stays work this way. Tick **Include the end date** when both days count, as for a holiday from Monday to Friday, which is five days off work. Legal deadlines usually leave out the first day and count the last. Rule 6 of the US Federal Rules of Civil Procedure says to "exclude the day of the event that triggers the period" and to "include the last day". That is the same as adding the days on the second tab. Under that rule a period that would end on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday runs on to the next day that is none of these. ## Counting only working days Working days leave out Saturdays, Sundays and the holidays you list. Open **Working days and holidays** to change the weekend, for example to Friday and Saturday, and to type holidays one per line. One button adds the US federal holidays. In Excel and Google Sheets, `=NETWORKDAYS(A1, B1)` counts both the start and the end date, so tick **Include the end date** to match it. `=WORKDAY(A1, 10)` does not count the start date, and neither does the second tab with **Count only working days** ticked. ## How many weeks is 90 days? Divide by seven: the whole part is the weeks and the remainder is the days left over. | Days | Weeks and days | |---|---| | 30 | 4 weeks, 2 days | | 45 | 6 weeks, 3 days | | 60 | 8 weeks, 4 days | | 90 | 12 weeks, 6 days | | 180 | 25 weeks, 5 days | | 365 | 52 weeks, 1 day | ## Why months give different day counts Months have 28 to 31 days, so "one month" is not a fixed number of days. One month after 15 January is 15 February, 31 days later. One month after 15 February is 15 March, only 28 days later, or 29 in a leap year. When the day does not exist in the new month, the calculator uses the last day of that month: 31 January plus one month is 28 February, or 29 February in a leap year. Years and months are added first, then weeks and days. For an age in years, months and days, use the [age calculator](/age-calculator/). The [week number](/week-number/) page shows the week of the year for any date, and the [hours calculator](/hours-calculator/) counts the time between two clock times. faq: - q: How many days are there until Christmas? a: Press the Christmas button under the dates. The calculator then counts from today to the next 25 December. On 1 December the answer is 24 days. On 26 December it is 364 days, or 365 when a 29 February falls in between. - q: What date is 90 days from today? a: Open Add or subtract days and press the 90 days button. The start date is already today. Ninety days is 12 weeks and 6 days, which is close to three months but not always the same date. 90 days after 14 November 2026 is 12 February 2027, while three months after it is 14 February 2027. - q: How many working days are there in a year? a: A year has 260 to 262 weekdays, Monday to Friday, before public holidays are taken off. 2026 has 261, which leaves 250 working days after the eleven US federal holidays. To count any year, enter 1 January and 31 December and include the end date. - q: How do I do this in Excel or Google Sheets? a: Subtract the cells. `=B1-A1` gives the days between two dates without the end date, and `=DAYS(B1, A1)` does the same. `=A1+45` adds days. `=EDATE(A1, 3)` adds months and, like this page, stops at the last day of a shorter month. - q: Are the dates I enter stored or sent anywhere? a: No. Everything is calculated by JavaScript in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or saved. Your dates are kept only in the page address after the # sign, a part that browsers do not send to the server, so you can bookmark or share a calculation. ---
