--- title: Word Counter description: Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time as you type. Free, instant, and private. Your text never leaves your browser. category: Text keywords: [word count, character count, character counter, letter counter, reading time, sentence counter] howto: | ## How to use the word counter Type or paste your text into the box. The counts update instantly as you type, so you can watch a tweet, essay, or meta description stay within its limit. - **Words** are runs of letters or numbers separated by spaces or punctuation. Hyphenated words like *well-known* and contractions like *don't* count as one word. - **Characters** counts every character, including spaces and line breaks. **Characters (no spaces)** leaves out all whitespace. - **Reading time** assumes 238 words per minute, the average silent reading speed for adults. **Speaking time** assumes 150 words per minute. ## Common length limits | Where | Limit | |---|---| | X (Twitter) post | 280 characters | | Google title tag | about 60 characters | | Meta description | about 155 characters | | SMS message | 160 characters (GSM-7) | | College essay (Common App) | 650 words | faq: - q: Is my text uploaded anywhere? a: No. The counting happens entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing you type is sent to a server or stored. - q: How are words counted? a: A word is any run of letters, digits, apostrophes, or hyphens. That matches how Microsoft Word and Google Docs count in almost every case. - q: Does it count emoji and accented characters correctly? a: Yes. Characters are counted as user-perceived characters, so an emoji or an accented letter counts as one character even when it is stored as several code units. ---
