Earlier moments are negative numbers: the Moon landing, 20 July 1969 at 20:17:40 UTC, is ``. The code carries no time zone at all: each reader's app formats that moment in their own time zone. The time zone you choose here is only used to turn the clock time you typed into that number. Around daylight saving changes some clock times happen twice and some never happen. For a repeated time the generator uses the first occurrence; for a skipped time (such as 2:30 AM on a spring-forward night) it moves forward by the length of the gap, and it tells you when either happens. ## Countdowns for events The `R` style is the one to use for anything upcoming: each reader's app recalculates it, so "in 2 hours" counts down by itself and becomes "2 hours ago" once the moment has passed.
