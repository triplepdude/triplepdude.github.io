That needs an alpha channel: | Format | Transparent corners | Good for | |---|---|---| | PNG | yes, lossless | profile pictures, logos, further editing | | WebP | yes, far smaller for photos | websites and apps | | JPG | no, filled with the background colour | sites that reject transparency | {: .data .circ-table} The edge is anti-aliased: pixels on the rim are partly transparent, so the outline looks smooth on any background instead of jagged. Besides the circle there is a **rounded square**, the shape of app icons and some profile photos, whose corner radius is a percentage of the width (20 to 25% gives an app-icon look), and a plain **square** for a square crop. ## How the crop is calculated At 100% zoom the shorter side of your image exactly spans the shape, so it is always filled. Dragging is limited so you cannot pull the image away from the edge. Zooming out below 100% shrinks the whole picture inside the shape; the uncovered area stays transparent or takes the background colour, which is handy for logos. The crop is stored relative to the shape, not in screen pixels, so the file matches the preview at every output size. The editor shows a lighter copy of large photos to stay fast, but the download is drawn from the original pixels with high-quality resampling. If the crop covers fewer source pixels than the output size, the image has to be enlarged and will look soft; the tool tells you when that happens. The photo's EXIF rotation is applied first, and no metadata, such as the GPS location, is copied to the new file. ## Choosing a size Profile photo slots are small on screen but are shown at two or three times the resolution on phones, so bigger files stay sharper: 256 px suits icons and chat avatars, 512 px most profile pictures, and 1024 px or more high-resolution profiles and prints. A border is drawn inside the shape, so the file stays exactly the size you chose; its width is in pixels of the file, so for 512 px, 8 to 16 px gives a clear ring. faq: - q: Is my photo uploaded anywhere? a: No. The image is loaded and cropped by your browser on this page.
