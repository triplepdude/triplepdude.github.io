TripleP Tools is a collection of small, fast, free utilities for developers and
everyday tasks: formatters, converters, calculators, and generators.

**Everything runs in your browser.** The tools are plain JavaScript that runs on
your own device. Text you paste, files you open, and numbers you enter are never
uploaded to a server, and there is no account to create.

The site is kept free by advertising. Ads never block a tool, and the tools work
the same with an ad blocker.

Found a bug or want a tool that isn't here? Open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/{{ site.github_username }}/{{ site.github_username }}.github.io/issues).
